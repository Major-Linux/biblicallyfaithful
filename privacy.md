## Third Party Services

### YouTube

Some pages on this site embed YouTube videos provided by Google LLC. When you play a YouTube video, YouTube may set cookies on your device to track viewing activity and preferences. This is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

You can view Google's privacy policy at: **https://policies.google.com/privacy**

If you prefer not to receive YouTube cookies, you can choose not to play embedded videos on this site.

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR you have the right to:

- Request deletion of your data
- Object to processing of your data

Since we collect no personal data, these rights are largely not applicable. However if you have any concerns please contact us.
