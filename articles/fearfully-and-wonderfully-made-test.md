God made each individually
   

The theory of Evolution would tell you that the universe was an accident, and that life starting on earth was by pure chance, and that we are nothing more than chemicals, continuing to live and evolve with no reason and purpose. Yet God tells us differently in his Holy and preserved scriptures.
 
Even before the moment you are conceived, God has determined who you are and set about creating you, an individual and precious soul, made in his very image.
 
All you parts are logged and then knitted together (fashioned), to become a unique individual.
    
The rich and poor meet together: the LORD is the maker of them all.  
**Proverbs 22:2**
   

He that oppresseth the poor reproacheth his Maker: but he that honoureth him hath mercy on the poor.  
**Proverbs 14:31**
    
Did not he that made me in the womb make him? and did not one fashion us in the womb?  
**Job 31:15**
