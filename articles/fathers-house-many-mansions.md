> [!Scripture]
In my Father's house are many mansions: if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you.
**John 14:2**  

 
> [!quote]
> Some general quotation from a book, sermon, or person — not scripture.
> **— C.S. Lewis**


   
Many think this verse is talking about the new heavenly abode of the righteous, a large, even luxurious mansion in which to live the sweet life, but this is not in context with the passage.  
   
To understand the context for this verse we need to go back to the end of the previous chapter. John 13:31+  
   
Jesus has just given Judas permission to go, knowing he is going to be betrayed. Now Jesus is left with the faithful (which includes Simon Peter).  
   
From here the whole conversation is about Jesus going away, and where he is going they cannot go… (that is, crucifixion, death then hell, before being raised from the dead)  
Jesus then goes on to talk about not leaving them as orphans (Holy Spirit – the comforter)  
Then promising his return in the future and receiving them to himself. (Rapture)  
   
   
So then, in context the mansions are prepared places in the Kingdom of Heaven, including a place for their name to be written in the Book of Life.
