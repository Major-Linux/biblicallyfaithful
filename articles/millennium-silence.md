Nobody else in the NT talks of a thousand year reign..... NOBODY. They all talk of Jesus coming back, but there is not one word spoken about Christ coming back for a thousand year period.
 
There is no teaching on how to prepare for such a time, what to do and what to expect..... NOTHING. There is plenty of talk of keeping or being kept till the day of Christ.
   

## Example scripture evidence:  


> [!Scripture]
“Nevertheless I am not ashamed: for I know whom I have believed, and am persuaded that he is able to keep that which I have committed unto him against that day.” 
**2 Timothy 1:12**




> “Being confident of this very thing, that he who hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ” 
**Philippians 1:6**
