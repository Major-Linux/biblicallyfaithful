## A Verse to Start

The word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword.


**All** **scripture is inspired of God**
 
Many believe that only the original autographs were inspired. Paul in 2 Tim 3:16 tells us that **ALL** scripture is inspired, that would include all of the OT, of which NO original autographs would have been in existence at the time Paul wrote his letter to Timothy. As all scripture when used regularly, even if carefully handled, only has a short life span. Because of this scribes would make regular copies. At the time of writing all the manuscripts that Paul was referring to Timothy that were suitable for " doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness", where copies of copies of copies, and therefore copies have the same authority as that originally penned
   

Additionally, it makes no logical sense to say that, even if a copy is IDENTICAL to the original, it no longer has the authority of the original and cannot be considered inerrant.

# Inspired authorship only?
 
does it really make sense for god to inspire the writers of the old and new testament books intended to preserve it but not inspired the translator's is so that they would get it wrong how would you expect a word to peter several generations
 
How would God preserve his word if he could not or would not ensure accurate copies and translations. It defies logic.
 
Why limit the scripture ALL scripture is inspired of God, to one copy and one author? Many of those that God inspired for scripture did not actually write/pen the writings.
