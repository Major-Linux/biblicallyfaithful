  How much of the scipture should you trust? Which bit's can you trust?

  Almost all Bible versions will contain a verse that retains much of the same meaning regardless of translation, and yet theologians, academics and proponents of those modern Bible versions, will tell you we don't have the original manuscripts and copying errors have been introduced over the centuries, so we can only have an aproximation of what the originals might have actually said. 

  So what is this verse that all Bible version agree on?

> [!Scripture]
"All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness. "
**2 Tim 3:16-17**
