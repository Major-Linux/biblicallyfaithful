# About Biblically Faithful
Below is a brief summary of the objectives and mission goals for this ministry.


## Statement of Faith

- There is a God
- Jesus Christ is the son of God
- The Bible speaks of
	- God’s nature    
    - God’s will    
    - God's plan of salvation and eternal life for mankind
- God is ready to forgive **all** our sins because Jesus, laid down his life as a sacrifice for **all**
- Salvation is available to **all** through faith in Jesus Christ



## God Has Provided Salvation for Those Who Believe

- By **grace** alone.    
- Through **faith** alone.    
- In **Christ** alone.    
- According to **Scripture** alone.    
- For the **glory of God** alone.



## Objectives of the Ministry

- To help Christians grow in their faith and knowledge of God.    
- To help clarify the central teachings of the Christian faith.    
- To help Christians protect themselves against various dangers to their spiritual well-being.    
- To provide articles and video content that support and encourage the objectives outlined above.
