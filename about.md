# About Biblically Faithful

_**In Progress**: This page is till being put together, but it is deemed helpful to have a basic draft here to serve as an outline._

## Statement of faith

- There is a God
- Jesus Christ is the son of God
- The Bible speaks to
	- God’s nature    
    - God’s will    
    - The promise of eternal life with God
- God provides forgiveness of our sins and through Jesus
- Salvation is available to all through faith in Christ

## Objectives of ministry

- To help Christians grow in their faith and knowledge of God
- To help provide clarity in the central teachings of the faith
- To help Christians protect themselves against various dangers to their spiritual wellbeing
- Providing articles and video content to encourage the points outlined above
