# What is a Biblical church?

Notice within this chapter of Matthew 16 the sequence of events

**First**, it is discussed who do the people think Jesus is

**Second**, Simon Peter declares Jesus as the son of God

**Third**, Jesus makes the declaration and promise… upon THIS rock I will build my church. (As already stated in the published article, this is referring back to Peter's statement of faith in Jesus as the Christ)

**Forth**: Jesus rebukes Satan's continued attempt to subvert Jesus from his mission

**Fifth**: Jesus goes on to qualify what is required to enter the kingdom of heaven and receive salvation
    
The whole chapter has been about Jesus, his divinity, mission and salvation

**Notes**  
The Rock is not Jesus as many suppose, the church is NOT JUST Jesus (He would not need anyone else, that being the case).

Jesus is the head of the Church, the body of Christ is the Church.

The focus of the passage of Matthew 16 is NOT Jesus, but Peter's confession of faith in Jesus being the Christ, the son of God, and it is upon THAT CONFESSION, that Jesus has and continues to, build his church.


The rock was not Peter, because Peter is dead, the church is alive forevermore

The rock is not built on Peter, as a few moments later Peter & Satan were rebuked for rebuking him, and trying to deter Jesus from his mission
